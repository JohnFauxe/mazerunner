MazeRunner Viewer

Placeholder (phase 2). Planned layout:

  • Canvas: draws cells from header.geometry (square, polar arcs, hex, …) and applies events.
  • Transport: play / pause / step ± / scrub bar / speed (1–1000 events/s) / jump to phase.
  • Sources: open a .trace.jsonl (replay) or connect to /api/live (SSE, live).
  • Race mode: several solvers on the same maze, side by side, stepped in lockstep.
  • Counters panel: nodes expanded, frontier size, steps and revisits, updated per event.
  • Results tab (phase 3): charts from benchmark JSONL, click a row to replay that trial.
